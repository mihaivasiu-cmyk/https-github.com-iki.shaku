Flute sample bank (ro … hi, *_chu, *_dai)
=========================================
Source: the "Shakuhachi" sample pack by Samplephonics (Sustained folder, take 03),
cut to 4 s, levelled and re-encoded by tools/sample-import/build_sp_bank.py.

re-selling or distributing the products "in their original format". Embedding

permission from Samplephonics (or replace the bank with your own recordings)
before distributing a build that contains these files.

Previous banks are kept in assets/samples_riley (Riley Lee signature tune) and
assets/samples_nbm (NBM158 phrases); neither is cleared for release either.
